| OQ-21 | **Alpha from a container source.** Section 3 says raw EXR output keeps alpha when the source has a real one. Two things are undecided: what counts as a *real* alpha rather than an opaque one the codec always carries, and whether a plate consolidated out of Resolve ever legitimately has one. `MediaInfo` records no alpha field today, so nothing could set the flag even if the decoder took one. Answer with a real turnover alongside OQ-3 | RGB only from a container; alpha preserved on the EXR path. Decoding `gbrapf32le` is a one line change once there is something to switch on | no |

| OQ-23 | **VideoToolbox quality is uncalibrated.** Two halves. *Does the encoder open a session on real hardware?* **ANSWERED 2026-09-11 by CI**: `h264_videotoolbox` encoded 24 frames on a `macos-latest` arm64 runner and ffprobe read the result back as h264. | OQ-27 | **Does a turnover's wav start where its picture media starts?** The reference mp4 muxes the associated wav, and the editor may deliver a trimmed range of the clip, so the audio has to be seeked by the same amount or the sound runs ahead of the picture by the length of the trim. `render._audio_skip` drops `(in - src_start)` frames' worth, which assumes the wav and the plate share a start. If the wav turns out to carry its own start timecode, this becomes a timecode difference rather than a frame offset.
